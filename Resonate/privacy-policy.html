Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Overview

Rezonance ("the App") is a whole-mind focus app with generative visuals, optional binaural beats, soundscapes, selected music, and a meditation timer. The App is designed to work without accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, or server-side user profiles.

Data Collection

The App does not collect personal data during normal use.

  • No account or login is required.
  • The App does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, camera data, or microphone data.
  • The App does not use advertising or tracking frameworks.
  • The App does not sell or share personal data.
  • The App does not upload mandala artwork, settings, or audio selections to a server.

Local Storage

The App stores preferences locally so the experience persists between sessions. This includes mandala slider settings, seed and style options, the binaural pad position, and timer duration.

On web, these preferences are stored in browser local storage. On iOS and Android, they are stored in the app's local document or cache storage. This data stays on your device and can be removed by clearing site data or uninstalling the App.

Captures, Files, And Photo Library

If you choose to capture or save a mandala image, the App writes the generated PNG locally. If you choose "Save to Photos," the App requests photo-library permission and saves the image to your device. If you choose "Share," the App uses the platform share sheet.

The App does not read your photo library for normal use and does not upload saved images anywhere.

Audio

Binaural beats, soundscapes, preview audio, timer chimes, procedural audio buffers, and any platform fallback audio buffers are created or played locally on the device. If you choose music from your Apple Music library, Rezonance requests permission only to show and play the songs or playlists you select for the session. The App does not record audio and does not request microphone access.

Crash Diagnostics

The App does not send crash diagnostics to a third-party service in the current production configuration. Error details may be kept in local in-app diagnostics so you can choose to share them when reporting a bug.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social media services. Platform services such as Photos, local file storage, and sharing are used only for the user-facing functions described above.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App contains no user accounts, social features, user-generated content feeds, ads, or in-app purchases.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted with an updated revision date.

Contact
