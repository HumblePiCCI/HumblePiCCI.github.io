Rezonance Support

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Rezonance is a local generative art, binaural focus, and meditation timer app for iOS. This page is the public support entry point prepared for App Review.

Before You Contact Support

  • Confirm you are running the latest TestFlight or App Store build.
  • Restart the app if audio, controls, or image export appear stuck.
  • For audio issues, note whether you were using the phone speaker, wired or USB-C headphones, or Bluetooth.
  • For image export issues, confirm Photos permission is enabled for Rezonance in iOS Settings.

What To Include

  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • The Rezonance app version and build number.
  • The route you were using: speaker, wired or USB-C headphones, or Bluetooth.
  • The exact steps that reproduced the issue.
  • Whether the issue affects mandala visuals, image export, preview audio, Play/Pause, or the timer completion chime.

Support Contact

A public support contact address must be added before App Review submission. Do not mark the App Store support URL accepted until this page is hosted publicly and the final support contact is present.

Privacy

Rezonance does not require accounts, does not collect personal data during normal use, and does not use advertising or tracking frameworks. See the Privacy Policy for details.
